Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your upcoming visit, billing protocols, and clinic amenities.

We are contracted with all major South African medical schemes (Discovery, Bonitas, Momentum, Medscheme, Medihelp, GEMS). We submit claims electronically directly to your medical aid on your behalf immediately after your consultation. Please note that any co-payments, savings shortfalls, or non-autorised procedures are payable on the day via debit/credit card or Instant EFT.
Apex Dental & Medical Care offers secure underground parking with direct elevator access to our clinic floor, as well as dedicated shaded surface parking bays monitored by 24/7 CCTV and boom gate security. Parking validation is provided free of charge for all clinic patients at reception.
For your first visit, please bring your South African green ID book or smart ID card, your medical aid membership card, a list of any current prescription medications you are taking, and any previous dental X-rays or medical records if applicable. You will also be asked to complete a brief digital intake questionnaire upon arrival.
